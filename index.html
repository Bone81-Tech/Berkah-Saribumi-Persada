Our Process

Sustainable Cultivation

Our patchouli is grown by local farmers using eco-friendly practices. We work directly with farming communities in Indonesia to ensure fair livelihoods and environmental stewardship.

  • Organic farming methods

Precision Steam Distillation

Freshly harvested leaves undergo steam distillation in our modern facility. Controlled temperature and pressure preserve the delicate Patchouli Alcohol compounds that define premium quality.

  • High Patchouli Alcohol yield
  • Zero solvent residue
